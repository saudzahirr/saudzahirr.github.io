Internship

June 2022 – August 2022

This eight-week internship was approved by MEPCO headquarters and comprised three placements: the SS&T Division, Multan (27 June – 15 July), the Operation Division, Moosa Pak (18 July – 5 August), and the T&I Division of GSC, Multan (8 – 19 August).

The first placement included training at the 132 kV Wapda Town grid station. It covered the station’s single-line diagram, from the two incoming 132 kV lines and the three power transformers (one 31.5/40 MVA and two 20/26 MVA) down to the 11 kV feeders. It also covered the hourly log sheets of transformer temperatures, tap positions, feeder loads, SF6 breaker pressure and the 110 V DC battery, the protection settings of the 11 kV feeder panels, and the permit-to-work procedure for isolating and earthing equipment. Together, the three placements gave hands-on experience in transmission, distribution, grid maintenance, and utility operations.
